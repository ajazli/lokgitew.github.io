So… what is this?

We're skipping the swiping, the awkward DMs and the "so… what do you do?" situations.

LOK & LOVE is a curated blind-date night at LOK GITEW where 12 strangers come together, eat, talk, play a few games and see if there's a spark. Six guys, six girls, one long table, and a proper plate of food in front of everyone.

You'll get short one-on-one sessions with everyone at the table, then dinner and group games. Afterwards, we handle the matching quietly — no public rejections, no big reveal, no drama.

Because there are only 12 seats, every applicant is hand-picked to keep the table balanced on age, interests and what everyone's actually looking for.

Questions You're Probably Asking

Does applying mean I've got a spot?

Not yet. There are only 12 seats, so our team reviews every application and picks a group that fits well together. Only selected applicants get contacted.

How will I know if I'm selected?

We'll message you on WhatsApp using the number you give us. If you don't hear from us, it just means this round wasn't the right fit — you're very welcome to apply again for the next one.

When do I pay?

Only after you're invited. Selected applicants pay Rp199.000 in full within 24 hours of getting the invitation. Your seat is confirmed once payment lands — nothing is charged when you apply.

Who can join?

Anyone single and 21 or older. We build each table to be balanced on age, interests and what people are looking for.

What happens to my information?

The details and photos you send are used purely to review your application. Your contact information is never passed to another participant unless you both agree to it.

Is the food included?

Yes — your ticket covers a LokGitew rice bowl and a welcome drink, plus the mini date sessions, group games and the matching process afterwards.

Do I have to come alone?

Yes. Everyone comes solo — that's rather the point. You'll know nobody at the table when you sit down, and that's what makes it work.
